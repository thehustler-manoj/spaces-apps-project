# Abandoned but not Forgotten

A storytelling website for the NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026 (Hyderabad local event), made for the challenge "Abandoned but not Forgotten: Storytelling about NASA's Discarded Equipment on the Moon and Mars."

## About the project
Humans have left machines on other worlds: Apollo landing equipment and rovers on the Moon, and the rovers Spirit and Opportunity and the helicopter Ingenuity on Mars. This website tells their stories and shows why they still matter.

## What's inside
- Apollo sites on the Moon
- Spirit and Opportunity on Mars
- Ingenuity, the first helicopter to fly on another planet
- Why these machines are never forgotten
